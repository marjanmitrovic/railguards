What Features Does RailGuard Offer?

An app that warns about potential train approach using GPS, a crossing database and timetables — even when there’s no barrier or signal.

RailGuard Active

Runs in the background and monitors crossings.

Railway Crossing Alert

Alarm when approaching (< 500 m).

Risk Zone

The map highlights the danger area.

Train Nearby

Risk / train indicator (based on data).

Offline Mode

Works without internet connection.

Simple Control

Start/Stop with minimal distraction.
